## Reading Time

SeoTax estimates reading time from text, images, code blocks, and tables.

## Previous and Next Posts

Navigation favors posts in the same child category,
then the parent category, and finally the wider post collection.

## Series

Use the `series` front matter field and the `series` shortcode
to present an intentional reading order.
